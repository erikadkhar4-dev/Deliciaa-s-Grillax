Our Story

Where the char
meets the chill.

Tucked into Bathu Basti on Airport Road, Deliciaa's Grillax built its name on the sound of live coals and the smell of marinade hitting hot iron. It's the kind of room locals return to on a regular Tuesday and visitors book for a special night out — the staff know it, and it shows.

By day it's a relaxed lunch stop for biryani and curries; after dark, the lighting drops, the grill counter glows, and the round mirror wall catches every flicker of it. We also host conferences and private events, with full takeaway available if you'd rather bring the char home.
